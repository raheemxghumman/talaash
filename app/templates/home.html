Lost-child desk for malls, airports, stadiums and festivals

The first ten minutes decide how a lost child is found.

Most venues still run that search on radios and paper. Talaash lets AI do the coordination while people make every call that matters.

Report a missing child Staff: report a sighting

What the AI does

  • Turns a frightened parent’s words, in any language, into a clean case.
  • A second model from a different family checks it against the parent’s exact words.
  • Matches staff sightings to open cases and scores how sure it is.
  • Runs the clock: widens the search, then calls in the police.

What people decide

  • Nothing is broadcast until the desk officer approves it.
  • Unsure matches go to a person, never straight to the parent.
  • Only a person can close a case. Every alert is then withdrawn.
  • Every agent decision and human action is logged.
